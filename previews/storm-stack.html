Wobbly towers. Wild magic.

Pick your backdrop and a sidekick, then duel the computer or a friend on floating islands. Blocks rock, overhangs topple, and removing supports can bring down the tower. Your small wizard guides every piece in golden magic. Cross the rainbow diamond line and choose one of three Helping or Attack powers. Use X/C/V, click a bubble, or tap your phone choices. Reach the higher gold finish strip to win. Use arrow keys against the computer. On a phone, swipe sideways to move, swipe down to fall faster, and tap to rotate.
