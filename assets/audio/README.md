# City Builder pet recordings

Both recordings are distributed under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- `city-dog-bark.wav`: “Dog Barking” from [Random Sounds Samples](https://opengameart.org/content/random-sounds-samples), recorded by Augmentality (Brandon Morris), uploaded by HaelDB. Cropped from 0.95 to 3.05 seconds, mixed to mono, resampled to 22,050 Hz, normalized and given short fades; exported as 16-bit PCM WAV.
- `city-cat-meow.wav`: `cat_mewfood.wav` from [Cat Purr & Meow](https://opengameart.org/content/cat-purr-meow), recorded by Kerzoven. Original recording, unchanged.

These files ship with the static site. Playing them does not contact a third-party server.
