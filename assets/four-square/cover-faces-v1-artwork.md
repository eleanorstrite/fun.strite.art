# Cover-based Four Square character faces

`cover-faces-v1.png` was generated with the built-in imagegen tool using the existing `../game-covers/four-square-still-v3.webp` as its identity and design reference. It supplies reconstructed face textures for curved 3D heads. The original cover remains unchanged. The four columns are King, Queen, Knight and Peasant. Runtime costumes and motion are separate 3D geometry. The coach uses a face crop from the original cover.

Generation prompt:

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: high-resolution face reference texture atlas for the actual 3D Four Square game characters. Reference image is the existing game cover; preserve the identities of its FOUR CHILDREN, their natural child facial proportions and their photorealistic rendered appearance. Output ONE wide horizontal atlas with four equal-width columns. In order from left to right: the curly brown-haired KING BOY from upper left; the curly brown-haired QUEEN GIRL from upper right; the brown-haired KNIGHT BOY from lower right; the brown-haired PEASANT BOY from lower left. These must look like the same real-looking children on that cover, not cartoon substitutes. Each column shows one complete head and short neck, DIRECTLY FRONT FACING with level head and eyes looking toward camera. Include the full scalp, naturally detailed brown hair, both ears, forehead, cheeks, chin and a short neck. Hair styles match the reference identities: the king has short rich brown curls, the queen longer brown ringlet curls at the sides, knight short brown curls, peasant short soft brown waves. Remove crowns, helmets, hats, capes, jewelry, collars, and clothing from these face-only texture assets because the game supplies those separately. Each head is equally sized and symmetrically placed within its own column with ample transparent separation. Genuine transparent background. Friendly subtle smile with relaxed lips, warm approachable natural eyes of realistic size. Match the cover's warm skin tones, fine skin detail, brows, lashes and believable nose/mouth forms. Soft even neutral frontal lighting suitable for texture projection onto curved 3D heads, very subtle shadows, no strong directional shadows. No body, no hands, no multiple views, no titles, no numbers, no text, no outlines, no logos. Crucial: realistic child faces consistent with the exact cover, no chibi proportions, no doll eyes, no painted-on oversized round pupils, no plastic bobblehead appearance.

## Costume materials

`cover-costume-materials-v1.png` was generated with the built-in imagegen tool using the same unchanged cover. Its four quadrants supply blue/gold royal brocade, ivory/gold floral fabric, orange/gold cape fabric and engraved silver/gold metal.

Material generation prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: game material texture atlas for the clothing and armor of the Four Square children on the reference cover. Produce ONE square 2 by 2 grid texture atlas with four exactly equal square quadrants, edge-to-edge material only. No people, no limbs, no faces, no outfits, no lettering, no labels, no borders, no symbols except the embroidered ornamental designs. Every quadrant is a straight-on flat close-up of its material in even soft neutral lighting, without strong lighting gradients, with fine realistic surface detail. Top left: the King's rich dark royal blue velvet brocade with small ornate gold embroidered fleurs-de-lis and curling gold vine motifs, matching the cover. Top right: the Queen's warm ivory fabric with delicate small gold embroidered flower-and-leaf vines, matching the cover. Bottom left: the Queen's warm orange-gold velvet cape fabric with fine gold scrolling leaf embroidery, matching the cover. Bottom right: the Knight's realistic bright silver brushed steel armor surface, with subtle gold etched curling floral ornament, small fine scratches and physically believable metal detail, matching the cover. All four patterns should repeat consistently and cover the entire quadrant; no large central emblems. No wrinkles that look like limbs, no separate objects, no perspective, no depth scene. This is a material swatch atlas for actual animated 3D costumes, not an image of characters. Closely match the cover's blue, ivory, orange-gold and silver-gold materials.
