Built-in image generation, material atlas used on the actual playable 3D models.

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: game-ready material texture atlas, exactly FOUR columns by TWO rows, eight equal square tiles, photographed perfectly flat and straight-on with soft neutral even illumination, NO text, labels, perspective, objects, cast shadows, frames or gutters. Each texture fills its entire tile and is independently seamless/tileable. Row one left to right: 1 warm cream irregular limestone cobblestone paving with fine mortar and light wear, 2 pale warm ivory limewashed stucco with natural mineral grain, 3 rich burnt terracotta overlapping curved roof tiles with subtle handmade variation, 4 honey-colored oak planks with fine realistic wood grain and narrow joints. Row two left to right: 1 warm ivory woven linen fabric with visible fine threads, 2 deep caramel brown worn leather with fine pores, 3 soft mossy forest-green foliage/leaf microtexture filling the entire square, 4 warm cream lightly veined honed marble countertop. These will be mapped directly onto cozy realistic 3D game buildings, furniture and clothing. Make material surfaces richly detailed but avoid harsh lighting or baked-in highlights.
